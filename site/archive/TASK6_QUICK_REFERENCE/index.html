    Database Structure¶

    Each JSON file in switching_pairs/ follows the SwitchingPair schema:

    SwitchingPair
├── pair_id: str
├── pair_type: str (mosfet_self, igbt_self, etc.)
├── high_side: PairSide
│   ├── device_id: str
│   ├── metadata: DeviceMetadata
│   ├── electrical_ratings: DeviceElectricalRatings
│   ├── conduction: ConductionData
│   ├── capacitance: CapacitanceData
│   └── gate_charge: list
├── low_side: PairSide (same structure)
├── switching_data: SwitchingData
│   ├── source: str (datasheet, dpt_measurement, etc.)
│   ├── turn_on: SwitchingEnergyData
│   ├── turn_off: SwitchingEnergyData
│   └── reverse_recovery: SwitchingEnergyData
└── migration: MigrationMetadata

    Quality Score Calculation¶

    Quality scores are calculated in pair_models.py._calculate_quality_score():

    • Metadata (30 pts): name, type, manufacturer
    • Electrical ratings (20 pts): v_abs_max, i_abs_max
    • Conduction data (20 pts): curves present
    • Switching data (30 pts): turn_on/off current axes
    • Thermal (10 pts): r_th_jc present

    Scoring: points / max_points * 100
